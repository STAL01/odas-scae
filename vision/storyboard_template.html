      4 · To build

      What changes in the code

      • Real places on the map. The mission starts as Sofia → Ruse with these stops. An "Add stop" button lets you click any other place and give it a name and a kind (airport, road, rail, bridge, port).
      • Gimbal camera. When the balloon passes within 15 km of a stop, the camera points at it and takes the picture from the real satellite imagery of that spot, at the resolution the slant range allows.
      • Operator camera view. Rotated boxes with labels, the overlay shown above, a scale bar, and filters for vehicles, infrastructure, and aircraft and ships.
      • All detector classes. Planes, helicopters, bridges, roundabouts, storage tanks and harbours are shown, not only vehicles.
      • Intel report tab. One line per capture with the position of every finding, exported as a spreadsheet file and as KML for Google Earth.
      • Route check. The planner says when a stop is out of reach of the wind, so you move the launch site or the target before the demo.

      Your decisions
